## Overview

Quantum Monte Carlo methods are first-principle electronic structure approaches placed at the high end of the accuracy ladder, and among the few approaches in quantum simulations that can fully exploit upcoming exascale supercomputers. The workshop, held online in January 2023, targeted Ph.D. students and young researchers with no previous experience of QMC methods, with lectures and hands-on tutorials on LUMI. CHAMP was developed in the context of the TREX Centre of Excellence.
